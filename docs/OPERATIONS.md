# Launch operations

## Enquiry to sale

1. The customer creates an availability request. Confirm that its ID exists before continuing the WhatsApp conversation.
2. Staff revalidate delivery and payment details, then confirm once. Confirmation deducts stock and creates the immutable invoice snapshot.
3. Cancel pending enquiries without a stock movement. Use return only for a confirmed sale; it writes a reversal movement once.
4. Record a reason for every manual adjustment and review the audit ledger daily during launch week.

## Tally handoff

1. Resolve every missing or unsupported HSN/GST classification with the accountant.
2. Prepare a batch; keep Prepared, Downloaded and Confirmed Imported as distinct states.
3. The named operator downloads and imports the files into a copy of the Tally company first.
4. Confirm Imported only after Tally reconciliation. Download alone is not synchronization.
5. Test one Karnataka invoice, one interstate invoice and one return before production enablement.

## Product onboarding

Use stable SKUs shared by the website, native WhatsApp catalogue and Tally. Preview validation errors before explicit commit. Never turn a Coming Soon row into a priced item without adding reviewed SKU, tax, stock, copy and imagery data.
